# Deal Flow Skill — Stage Definitions and Exit Criteria

## Purpose

Define the pipeline stages, exit criteria, and transition rules for portfolio
projects. The portfolio-agent uses these definitions to assess whether a project
should advance, stay, or be flagged.

## Stages

### Contact (1-2 weeks)

**Goal:** Initial discovery and qualification.

**Exit criteria:**
- Team composition understood (founders, key hires)
- Product or service clearly described with current status
- Market segment identified
- Initial fit assessment recorded in Mimir or Paperclip

**Red flags if stuck:** No meeting scheduled after 7 days, no response after 2 outreach attempts.

### Diligence (2-4 weeks)

**Goal:** Deeper market, product, team, and reference work.

**Exit criteria:**
- Market size and competition analysis complete
- Product demo or walkthrough completed
- At least 2 customer or partner references checked
- Financial model or unit economics reviewed
- Team background checks complete

**Red flags if stuck:** Checklist items incomplete after 3 weeks, founder non-responsive.

### Decision (1-2 weeks)

**Goal:** Investment or program admission decision.

**Exit criteria:**
- Investment committee or decision meeting held
- Clear outcome recorded (proceed, reject, defer)
- Next contractual step identified (term sheet, program agreement, etc.)

**Red flags if stuck:** Decision meeting not scheduled after 5 business days.

### Acceleration (3-6 months)

**Goal:** Resource deployment and milestone tracking.

**Exit criteria:**
- Program milestones defined and tracked
- Resources actively deployed (mentors, connections, funding)
- Regular check-ins happening (at least monthly)
- Clear continuation or graduation criteria set

**Red flags if stuck:** No milestone progress for 30+ days, missed 2+ check-ins.

### Exit (Ongoing)

**Goal:** Graduation, alumni support, pivot, or discontinuation.

**Exit criteria:**
- Graduation criteria met, OR
- Mutual decision to discontinue, OR
- Pivot to new direction with updated tracking

**The project no longer needs active pipeline management.**

## Transition Rules

1. Never advance a project without evidence that exit criteria are met.
2. Cite specific evidence when recommending advancement.
3. If exit criteria are partially met, recommend specific actions to close gaps.
4. Stage regression is allowed when evidence shows a project was advanced prematurely.
